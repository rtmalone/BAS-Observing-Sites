Welcome to BAS Observing Sites

Welcome to the Barnard Astronomical Society's collaborative observing site directory! This platform allows our community to share and discover the best stargazing locations in the Chattanooga area.

Why Share Your Sites?

  • Help fellow astronomers find quality observation spots
  • Build a comprehensive map of local dark sky (dark-ish?) locations
  • Share valuable insights about site conditions and access

Each location you add helps expand our collective knowledge and strengthens our astronomical community. Share your favorite spots, from hidden urban gems to remote dark sky havens.
